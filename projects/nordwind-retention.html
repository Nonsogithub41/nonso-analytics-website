Customer-retention system — Nordwind

An end-to-end customer-retention system for an e-commerce store (“Nordwind”). It cleans the raw sales, scores every customer, measures who is drifting away, shows it on a live Power BI dashboard, and re-runs itself every week on GitHub — opening an alert with an AI-written briefing whenever the revenue at risk climbs too high.

Why it matters

Turns a pile of raw sales into one number a manager can act on — £1.32M of revenue sitting with 1,708 customers who are slipping away — then refreshes it, alerts on it, and explains it in plain English every week, with no one touching it.

01The problem

  • Winning a new customer costs far more than keeping one, so the quiet killer for any online store is churn — customers who buy once or twice and vanish.
  • The business question: who is slipping away, how much money do they represent, and what should we do — answered automatically, not in a one-off spreadsheet.

02How the system works, end to end

Seven stages run as one automated pipeline — from raw data to an AI-written briefing.

  1. Clean 540,000+ raw transactions into trustworthy sales (Python / pandas).
  2. Score every customer by Recency, Frequency and Monetary value, and sort them into plain groups — Champions, Loyal, At Risk, Lost.
  3. Measure retention over time with a cohort analysis, and flag the revenue at risk (At Risk + Lost).
  4. Visualise it on a two-page Power BI dashboard — an executive overview and a retention deep-dive.
  5. Automate the whole run on GitHub Actions — a fresh cloud machine does it every week, with no clicks.
  6. Alert — when revenue at risk crosses a threshold, it opens an issue with the win-back customer list and emails it.
  7. Explain — an AI step (Google Gemini) writes a plain-language briefing of the week and the top 3 actions.

03The hands-off loop

  • Once it is set up, no one touches it: the pipeline runs on a schedule, publishes fresh data the dashboard reads, and files an alert only when it is needed.
  • A one-line fix in the pipeline flows straight through to the dashboard on the next refresh — one source of truth, no manual re-importing.
  • The alert is not just a number: an AI briefing on top explains the week in plain words and names the exact customers to call first.
AI briefing · auto-generated each run

“We crossed our warning threshold this week with £1.32M in revenue at risk; 1,708 accounts have gone quiet. Top 3 actions: call top at-risk buyers 15749 (£44,534) and 15098 (£39,916); send a “we miss you” campaign to all 643 at-risk customers; investigate why the biggest lost buyer, 12346 (£77,184), stopped ordering.”
